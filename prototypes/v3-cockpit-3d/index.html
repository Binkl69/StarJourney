StarJourney

Halcyon is five legs away. You're in the pilot's seat. Keep the Meridian flying, keep the crew breathing, and bring her down in one piece.

  • SteerDrag anywhere in the window. (Keyboard: arrows / WASD)
  • BoostHold the round button. Burns fuel 3× faster. (Shift)
  • Route powerEngines = speed. Shields = less damage. Life = slower O₂ loss.
  • PatchSpend a salvaged part to fix the hull or seal a leak. (E)
  • CollectFly through fuel, oxygen and parts.
  • SurviveHull or oxygen hits zero and it's over.
